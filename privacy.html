Hermes Personal Dashboard

Privacy information

Purpose and access

This personal-use integration accesses only accounts that its owner authorizes. The permissions granted to a connection determine which Google services and actions it can use. Supported tasks include finding, reading and summarizing email, calendar entries, contacts, documents and files. Separately enabled writing capabilities have separate access and approval controls.

Processing and storage

The integration runs on devices and servers controlled by its owner. Retrieved content can be included in the owner's requested assistant tasks, local records, caches and conversation history. When a task uses an external AI service, relevant content may be transmitted to that service under the owner's configured account, terms and settings. These information pages do not host account data, credentials, mailbox contents or documents.

The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. It is not a dataset collection service for training general-purpose AI models.

Access controls and removal

Credentials are kept outside public source and information pages. Read-only connections and separately protected writing connections retain their respective permissions. The owner can revoke the app's access through their Google Account connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized API access; it does not automatically delete previously saved records, conversation history or backups. Those remain subject to the owner's storage and deletion controls.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google authorization screen to contact the operator.
